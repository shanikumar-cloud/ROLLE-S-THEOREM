ROLLE'S THEOREM

Interactive Mathematics Animation

Mathematical Formula

f(a) = f(b) ⇒ f'(c) = 0

If a function is continuous on [a,b], differentiable on (a,b), and f(a)=f(b), then at least one point c exists where the tangent is horizontal.
